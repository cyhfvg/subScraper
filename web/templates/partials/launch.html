Quick Tips

  • Enter a domain like example.com or use a wildcard suffix such as example.* to fan out across configured TLDs.
  • Prefix with *. to scan a sub-scope, e.g., *.apps.example.com.
  • Provide a wordlist path if you want ffuf vhost brute-forcing; leave it blank to skip ffuf automatically.
  • Jobs queue safely when worker slots are full. Configure concurrency limits in Settings.
  • Targets reuse the shared state.json, so reruns pick up where they left off.
